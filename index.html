OUR INSTITUTION
Est. 1999

RCC Institute of
Information Technology

RCC Institute of Information Technology (RCCIIT) is a reputed engineering college located in Kolkata. It was established in 1999 with the aim of providing quality technical education in the fields of engineering and information technology.

The institute is affiliated with Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology (MAKAUT) and offers several undergraduate and postgraduate courses such as B.Tech, BCA, and MCA in different technical disciplines.

RCCIIT focuses on developing students' technical knowledge, problem-solving ability, and practical skills through laboratory work, projects, and academic training. The institute also provides modern classrooms, computer laboratories, and a library.

The institute also encourages participation in cultural programs, technical events, and sports activities, preparing students for careers in engineering, information technology, and other technical fields.
